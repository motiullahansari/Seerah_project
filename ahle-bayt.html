01

Understanding Ahle Bayt

Ahle Bayt means “people of the household”. The Qur’anic passage in Al-Ahzab 33:32–34 addresses the Prophet’s wives. Sahih Muslim 2424 also records Ali, Fatimah, Hasan and Husayn being gathered beneath his cloak.

Qur’an · Al-Ahzab 33:32–34Qur’an · Al-Ahzab 33:33Sahih Muslim 2424

This overview uses commonly cited Sunni seerah accounts. The scope of Ahle Bayt is discussed differently across Islamic traditions; notes identify differences relevant to this chapter.

02

The wives of the Prophet ﷺ

The standard list contains eleven wives. Khadijah and Zaynab bint Khuzaymah died during the Prophet’s lifetime; nine wives survived him. Qur’an 33:6 calls his wives the mothers of the believers.

Maria al-Qibtiyya

Maria was the mother of Ibrahim. The cited scholarly account describes her as an umm walad, an enslaved woman who bore her master a child, rather than a wife.

Islam Q&A · Maria al-Qibtiyya · 47572

Rayhana bint Zayd

Early accounts differ over whether Rayhana was a wife or a concubine. Her status is therefore noted separately. The label “umm walad” requires evidence of a child and is not used here.

Islamweb · Rayhana bint Zayd · 398938
03

His sons and daughters

The usual seerah account names three sons and four daughters. Their mother was Khadijah, except for Ibrahim, whose mother was Maria al-Qibtiyya. The sons died in childhood.

04

The narration of the cloak

Aisha reports that Hasan, Husayn, Fatimah and Ali joined the Prophet ﷺ beneath his cloak, after which he recited the purification passage of Qur’an 33:33. The report is Sahih Muslim 2424.

Banu Hashim and the wider family

In Sahih Muslim 2408a, Zayd ibn Arqam names the families of Ali, Aqil, Ja‘far and Abbas while discussing the relatives prohibited from accepting zakat. This is a specific explanation within the report.

    علي بن أبي طالب

    Aqil ibn Abi Talib RACousin of the Prophet ﷺ
    عقيل بن أبي طالب
  3. 03
    Ja‘far ibn Abi Talib RACousin of the Prophet ﷺ

    Abbas ibn Abd al-Muttalib RAPaternal uncle of the Prophet ﷺ

Banu Hashim is the wider clan. These four entries are the families named in this report, and the list is not a census of the whole clan.
