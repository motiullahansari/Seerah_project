Privacy Policy & Terms

Privacy policy

No personal data collectedThe app does not ask for sign-up, login, name, email or phone number, and does not collect personal information.
Settings stay on your phoneLanguage, theme, text size, reminder time and quiz progress are saved only on your device.
Downloads stay inside the appOffline books are stored privately in the app, for reading inside the app only, and can be removed any time in Downloads.

Links to other appsWhatsApp, Telegram, YouTube, email and other links follow those services’ own privacy policies.
ChildrenThe app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
