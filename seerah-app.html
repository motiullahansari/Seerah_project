FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the Seerah app free?

Yes. The app is completely free, with no paywall. It is supported by the community through UmmahTechs.

Which languages are available?

The app interface is available in 9 languages, and books like Ar-Raheeq Al-Makhtum are being published in 28+ languages.

Where does the content come from?

Every book and answer is taken from authentic, well-known sources and reviewed by our content team before it is published.

Is there an iPhone version?

The app is available on Android today. An iOS version is planned, in shaa Allah.

How can I help?

You can join as a translator, reviewer, designer or developer, or support the project with a donation.
