FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the Seerah app free?

Yes. The app is completely free, with no ads and no paywall.

Which languages are available?

The app itself is available in 9 languages. Books like Ar-Raheeq Al-Makhtum are available in 28+ languages, and more are being added.

Where does the content come from?

From authentic, well-known books and sources, used with the permission of their authors and publishers. Every text is proofread by our team, and you can report any mistake from inside the app.

Can I read without internet?

Yes. Download a book in the app (Settings → Downloads) and read it offline any time.

Is there an iPhone version?

The app is available on Android today. An iOS version is planned, in shaa Allah.

How can I help?

Join us as a translator, proofreader, designer or developer, or support the project. Open Join us or Support us in the app, or message us on WhatsApp.
