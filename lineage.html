Family & lineage

The family and lineage of Muhammadﷺ

His family through Fatimah, his lineage to ‘Adnan, and the reported genealogies beyond, each with its book references.

Family tree

The Prophet’s ﷺ descendants through Fatimah and ‘Ali, with one line through each grandson. It is not a complete list of his family or descendants. [F2]

Why Husayn’s line stops here

The cited sources record a disagreement over whether Muhammad al-Mahdi was a son of al-Hasan al-‘Askari and is the awaited Mahdi, so he is not shown. This applies to this line only; it does not mean the descendants of al-Hasan and al-Husayn ended here. [F3] [F5]
