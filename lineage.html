Why this branch stops here

The supplied branch stops at Hasan al-Askari. Claims that Muhammad al-Mahdi was his son and is the awaited Mahdi are disputed in the cited sources, so he is not included here. This stopping point applies only to this branch; it does not mean that all descendants of Hasan and Husayn ended here. [F3] [F4]
