About Human Relief Nepal

Humanitarian support built around dignity.

Human Relief Nepal is a non-governmental and non-profit social organization committed to serving vulnerable, marginalized and disadvantaged communities.

Our approach combines immediate humanitarian response with longer-term support that helps communities recover, strengthen resilience and pursue sustainable opportunities.

Qurbani, Adak & Aqiqah

Sacrifices performed in Nepal, with the meat distributed to families in need. Contact our team to arrange yours.

☪

Eid al-Adha Qurbani

Your Qurbani is performed during the days of Eid al-Adha, and the meat is shared with vulnerable families.

  1. 1Contact us with your name and the number of shares.
  2. 2We confirm the arrangements and record your intention.
  3. 3The sacrifice is performed on the days of Eid and the meat is distributed.
